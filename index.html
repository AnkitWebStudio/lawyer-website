Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. Don't see yours? Get in touch directly.

How do I schedule a consultation?

You can schedule a consultation by filling in the contact form on this website, calling the office directly, or sending an email. We aim to respond within 24 hours and will arrange a time that is convenient for you — in person or by phone.

Which practice areas do you cover?

The firm handles a broad range of legal matters including Criminal Law, Civil Litigation, Family Law, Property Disputes, Consumer Matters, Corporate Law, Legal Documentation, and General Legal Consultation. Please see the Practice Areas section for full details.

What documents should I bring to my consultation?

Bring any documents relevant to your matter — court notices, FIR copies, property papers, agreements, correspondence, identification documents, or any other paperwork connected to your case. Even if you're unsure, bring everything you have and we'll guide you on what is important.

Are consultations confidential?

Absolutely. All consultations are strictly confidential and protected by attorney-client privilege. No information you share will be disclosed to any third party without your explicit consent.

How can I contact your office?

You can reach the office by phone at [Phone Number Placeholder], by email at [Email Placeholder], or by visiting us at [Office Address Placeholder], Firozpur, Punjab. Office hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Do you offer services outside Firozpur?

Yes. While the primary practice is based in Firozpur, we represent clients before the Punjab & Haryana High Court, as well as other tribunals and forums across Punjab. Please contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
